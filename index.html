Drop a PDF here or use “Open PDF”. Everything happens in your browser; your file is never uploaded. Need more detail? Click Help.

Flatten crops
Checkbox next to Export. Off: a crop only hides the outside area, and the hidden content is still inside the file. On: cropped pages are exported as images and everything outside the crop is permanently deleted (text on those pages can no longer be selected).
Pages with markup or redactions are exported as flattened images, so redacted text is truly removed. Untouched pages keep their selectable text. Cropping only hides the outside area unless “Flatten crops” is ticked.

How to use PDF Studio

Getting started

  • Click Open PDF or drop a file on the page. Your PDF is processed in your browser and never uploaded.
  • Change pages with the arrow buttons (or the ← → keys). Marks belong to the page you drew them on.
  • Zoom with − / + / Fit, by pinching with two fingers, or with Ctrl + mouse wheel. Zoom in for precise work like redaction boxes.
  • When you're done, click Export PDF (or Export as image). The original file is never changed.

Tools

Move
Scrolls and pans the page without marking it. Handy on touchscreens, where one finger normally draws.
Select
Click a mark to select it (dashed outline). Drag it to move it. Drag a square handle to resize shapes, highlights, redaction boxes and pictures. Boxes and ellipses also have side handles that stretch in one direction, and lines and arrows have a handle on each end. Hold Shift on a corner to keep proportions. Shapes and pictures have a round handle to rotate them (Shift = 15° steps), and the Rotate 90° button turns them a quarter turn. Press Delete or use the Delete button to remove a mark. Pen strokes, marker strokes and text can be moved but not resized or rotated.
Highlight
Drag a box to add a see-through color highlight. Choose color and opacity in the options bar.
Marker
Draw a freehand highlighter stroke. Options: color, size, opacity and tip (Chisel, Round, Soft, Square).
Pen
Draw freehand. Options: color, size, opacity and style (Round, Calligraphy, Dashed, Dotted).
Shapes
Drag to draw an Arrow, Line, Box or Ellipse; pick the shape from the dropdown. Boxes and ellipses can be Filled. Hold Shift (or tick Snap) for 45° lines and perfect squares or circles. To move, resize or rotate a shape afterward, use the Select tool.
Text
Click or tap where you want the text, type, then press Enter (or click elsewhere) to place it. Esc cancels. Choose font, bold, italic, color and size first.
Image
Click Image and choose a picture (PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF), or paste one with Ctrl/⌘ + V, or drop one onto the page. It appears in the middle of your view and is selected: drag it to move it, drag a corner handle to resize it (proportions stay locked), drag the round handle above it to rotate it (hold Shift for 15° steps) or click Rotate 90°, and press Delete to remove it. Transparent PNGs keep their transparency. Pages with images are exported as flattened images, like other marks.
Redact
Drag a solid black box over anything you want to hide. When you export, pages with redactions are turned into images, so the text underneath is permanently removed, not just covered.
Crop
Drag around the area you want to keep. Each page has its own crop; drag again to replace it, or use Reset crop. Nothing is cut until you export.

Undo and deleting

  • Undo (or Ctrl/⌘ + Z) reverses your last change on any page, including moves, resizes, deletes and crops.
  • Reset crop removes the crop from the current page.

Exporting

  • Pages with marks or redactions are saved as flattened images. That is what makes redactions permanent, but the text on those pages can no longer be selected.
  • Pages you didn't mark keep their selectable text.
  • Flatten crops (checkbox next to Export): off = a crop only hides the outside area, and the hidden content is still inside the file; on = cropped pages become images and everything outside the crop is permanently deleted.
  • Export as image saves the current page, or every page in a ZIP file, as PNG or JPG at 72, 144 or 216 dpi. Marks, redactions and crops are included.
  • For anything sensitive, use Redact, and check the exported file before sharing it.

Touchscreens

  • Draw with one finger. Use two fingers to pinch-zoom and pan, or switch to Move to scroll with one finger.
  • To rotate a picture or shape on a touchscreen, drag its round handle or use the Rotate 90° button.
  • The toolbar scrolls sideways on small screens; Export PDF stays pinned on the right.

Export as image

Your marks, redactions and crops are included. Several pages are saved together as one ZIP file.
